## 1. Integration Management

This comprehensive Project Management Plan integrates all subsidiary management plans to govern the execution, monitoring, and closure of the Campus Ignite system.

The plan establishes baselines for scope, schedule, and cost while defining rigorous quality, risk, and communications governance.

- Direct and manage project execution through agile iterative development sprints.
- Manage project knowledge and documentation within structured version-controlled repositories.
- Perform integrated change control across all application components.

## 2. Subsidiary Management Plans Summary

The following subsidiary plans guide project delivery across specific disciplines.

| Subsidiary Plan | Core Objective | Primary Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope Management | Deliver functional modules without scope creep | Project Lead |
| Schedule Management | Achieve milestone deliveries within sprint windows | Technical Lead |
| Quality Management | Ensure zero regression through automated test suite | QA Lead |

## 3. Performance Baselines & Review Gates

Milestone stage gates require explicit review and sign-off prior to advancing project phases. The project lead conducts weekly burndown reviews and automated test verification.
