## 1. User Acceptance Testing Scope

UAT ensures that the system satisfies the daily ministry workflows of pastors, administrators, and cell facilitators in a real-world campus environment.

| Participant | Assigned Workflow / Scenario | Target Acceptance Criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Senior Pastor | Review weekly consolidated reports and sermon history | Clean PDF exports and accurate headcount roll-ups |
| Campus Cell Leader | Submit weekly cell report and check birthday alerts | Report successfully submitted in under 2 minutes |
| Department Head | Create department event and view team roster | Event displays immediately on public ministry calendar |
