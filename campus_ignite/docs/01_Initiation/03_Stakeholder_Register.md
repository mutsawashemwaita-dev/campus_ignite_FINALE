## 1. Stakeholder Identification & Classification

The following stakeholders have been identified, assessed, and mapped according to their organizational influence and interest in the Campus Ignite platform.

| Stakeholder | Role / Group | Influence | Interest | Engagement Strategy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pastoral Team | Senior ministry oversight | High | High | Weekly briefings & consolidated report sign-off |
| Executive Leadership | Campus governance & ministry board | High | High | Monthly milestone reviews & strategy alignment |
| Cell Leaders | Campus cell fellowship facilitators | Medium | High | Interactive training, mobile-first report submission |
| Department Heads | Choir, media, ushering & logistics | Medium | Medium | Department member rosters & event scheduling |
| Student Anchors | Senior student mentors and leaders | Medium | High | Directory access and cell pastoral support |
| General Members | Congregation and campus students | Low | Medium | Self-service profile and public ministry calendar |
| IT / DevOps Lead | Mutsawashe Mwaita | High | High | Architecture, security hardening, continuous deployment |

## 2. Communication Requirements

Each stakeholder group requires tailored notification channels to maintain operational transparency.

- Pastors receive automated notifications when weekly consolidated cell reports are submitted.
- Cell leaders receive birthday alerts and report due date notifications.
- Department heads receive member joining notices and calendar event alerts.
