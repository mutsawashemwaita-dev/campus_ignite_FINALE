## 1. Strategic Context

Campus Christian Institution operates across multiple university campuses, student residences, and faculties. As the student fellowship expands, decentralized paper forms and WhatsApp messaging groups lead to lost attendance records, delayed pastoral follow-ups, and lack of visibility into student spiritual health.

## 2. Problem Statement

Manual coordination results in fragmented communications, inaccurate headcount metrics, missed first-timer follow-ups, and excessive administrative burden on pastoral leadership.

- Delayed or missing cell meeting reports from campus residences.
- Unreliable headcount tracking for weekly Sunday services.
- Inability to track evangelism outreach recruits through their salvation and discipleship journey.

## 3. Options Analysis & Recommendation

Three operational approaches were evaluated prior to platform selection.

| Option | Description | Cost / Feasibility | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Option A: Status Quo | Continue using WhatsApp groups and paper spreadsheets | Zero initial cost, high error rate, unscalable | Rejected |
| Option B: Commercial SaaS | Purchase generic church management software (e.g., Breeze, Planning Center) | High monthly subscription, lacks campus cell structure | Rejected |
| Option C: Custom Campus Ignite | Build modern Python/Django platform tailored to campus cells and student ministries | Low maintenance cost, exact fit, full institutional ownership | Recommended & Approved |

## 4. Financial & Operational ROI

The recommended solution delivers significant operational time savings (over 15 hours per week of manual data aggregation eliminated) and complete institutional data sovereignty at near-zero operating infrastructure cost on Render and Neon PostgreSQL.
