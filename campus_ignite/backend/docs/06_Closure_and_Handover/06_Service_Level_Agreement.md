## 1. Service Level Objectives (SLOs)

Uptime Commitment: 99.9% target service availability during campus operating semesters.

Scheduled Maintenance Windows: Sundays 22:00 to 24:00 (outside fellowship hours).

| Incident Priority | Definition / Criteria | Target Response Time | Target Resolution Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 - Critical | System-wide outage or auth failure | < 1 Hour | < 4 Hours |
| P2 - Major | Core module failure (e.g., reports down) | < 2 Hours | < 12 Hours |
| P3 - Minor | Non-critical cosmetic or minor UI glitch | < 8 Hours | < 48 Hours |
| P4 - Request | Feature enhancement or configuration change | < 24 Hours | Next Sprint Release |
