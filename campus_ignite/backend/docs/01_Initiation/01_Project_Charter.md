## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

The Campus Ignite project authorizes the design, development, and deployment of an integrated institutional ministry management web platform for the Campus Christian Institution.

The system addresses critical challenges in campus cell group coordination, real-time Sunday service reporting, attendance analytics, pastoral care tracking, and consolidated reporting.

- Digitize and automate cell group reporting and meeting summaries.
- Establish a centralized directory of leadership, pastoral teams, student anchors, and alumni.
- Provide real-time visibility into weekly service attendance, headcount trends, and evangelism outreach.
- Deliver birthday alerts and automated pastoral care notifications.

## 2. Project Objectives & Success Criteria

Measurable targets have been defined to validate the success and adoption of Campus Ignite across campus.

| Objective | Target Metric | Verification Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cell Reporting Modernization | 100% of campus cells submit weekly reports digitally | Database audit log |
| Leadership Accessibility | Single directory for pastors, anchors, and cell leaders | Admin verified roster |
| System Availability | >= 99.9% uptime on cloud production hosting | Render uptime monitoring |
| Response Time | Page loads under 500ms on desktop and mobile | Lighthouse & server metrics |

## 3. Scope Boundaries & Assumptions

In Scope: User authentication, RBAC, cell fellowships, departments, Sunday services, calendar, evangelism, notifications, PDF reporting.

Out of Scope: Financial payroll integration, third-party SMS gateway billing (using in-app and email notifications).

- Assumption: Users have internet access on campus via mobile devices or laptops.
- Assumption: Pastoral leadership provides weekly data for consolidated report review.
