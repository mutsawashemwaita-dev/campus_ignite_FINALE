## 1. Business Needs & Context

Campus Ignite addresses the organizational requirements of campus fellowship leadership to streamline administrative operations, improve data accuracy, and foster spiritual community engagement.

## 2. Functional Business Requirements

The system fulfills specific business needs classified by ministry operational domain.

| Requirement ID | Operational Domain | Business Rule / Capability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BR-01 | Member Directory | Maintain searchable profiles for pastors, anchors, leaders, and alumni. |
| BR-02 | Cell Coordination | Cell leaders must submit meeting reports with headcounts and offerings. |
| BR-03 | Service Records | Sunday preachers, sermon titles, and total attendee headcounts must be logged. |
| BR-04 | Department Operations | Department heads must track team members and schedule rehearsals/meetings. |
| BR-05 | Evangelism Tracking | New converts must be logged with assigned discipleship follow-up contacts. |
| BR-06 | Consolidated Reports | System must generate unified weekly PDF reports for pastoral executive review. |
| BR-07 | Automated Alerts | Notify leaders of pending reports, upcoming birthdays, and calendar events. |

## 3. User Personas & Workflows

Primary personas include: Senior Pastor (review analytics and consolidated reports), Cell Leader (manage members and submit reports), Department Head (coordinate ministry activities), and Student Anchor (mentor campus cells).
