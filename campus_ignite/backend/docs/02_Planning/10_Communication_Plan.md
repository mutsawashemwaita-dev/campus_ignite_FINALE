## 1. Communication Objectives & Channels

Ensures timely, transparent dissemination of information across technical developers, pastoral leaders, and campus fellowships.

| Channel | Audience | Frequency | Format / Medium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Technical Sprint Standup | Development & IT | Daily / Continuous | Git commits & automated test reports |
| Pastoral Operations Brief | Senior Pastors & Ministry Leads | Weekly | Consolidated PDF report & live dashboard |
| Cell Leader Reminders | Cell Facilitators | Weekly (Mondays) | In-app notifications & calendar reminders |
| Congregational Announcements | General Campus Fellowship | Sundays / Monthly | Public Calendar & announcements feed |

## 2. Escalation Procedure

Critical system bugs or downtime are escalated directly to Mutsawashe Mwaita with guaranteed resolution response within 2 hours.
